Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address and password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we also store the expense entries you add, including amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide the service: to sign you in, to show your expenses and spending summaries, and to keep your account working. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's database. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete individual expenses within the app, and you may request deletion of your account and its data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address according to Google's own privacy policy. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact the Spendly team using the contact details provided with the app.
